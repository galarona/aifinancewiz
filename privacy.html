Overview

FinAi ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. All financial data you enter or import into the App is stored and processed locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal or financial information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account registration is required to use the App.
  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number) are collected.
  • No usage analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers are collected.
  • No financial data (transactions, budgets, balances) leaves your device.

Financial Data

All transactions, budgets, categories, and related financial information you create in the App are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard data storage mechanisms. You can delete all of your data at any time by deleting the App.

On-Device Processing

AI-powered features, including spending analysis and the finance assistant, run entirely on your device. Your data is not sent to external servers for processing.

Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions, used solely for the features described:

  • Camera / Photo Library — used only to scan receipts or documents you choose to import. Images are processed on-device and are not uploaded.
  • Notifications — used only to deliver alerts you configure, such as budget reminders.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Purchases and subscriptions, if offered, are processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive access to your payment information.

Data Security

Because your data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's security features, including device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. We recommend keeping iOS up to date and using a device passcode.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected from any user, no children's data is collected.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
