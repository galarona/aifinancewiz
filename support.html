Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All of your financial data is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded to a server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data. You can also delete individual transactions and budgets from within the app.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone to view, change, or cancel your subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and choose the purchase you'd like refunded.

The app isn't working as expected. What should I do?

Try restarting the app and your device first. If the problem continues, email us at wavesupport2000@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your iOS version, and your device model.
